Sisig

Hailed as perhaps the greatest pork dish on earth, Sisig is a masterpiece of Kapampangan culinary ingenuity. Originating from Angeles City, Pampanga, the dish's name comes from the old Tagalog word 'sisigan', which means to make sour, traditionally referring to a salad of green fruits and vinegar.

The modern version we love today was revolutionized in the 1970s by Lucia "Aling Lucing" Cunanan. By using discarded pig heads from the nearby Clark Air Base and serving the chopped, seasoned meat on a sizzling stone plate, she transformed a simple snack into a national treasure. Today, Sisig represents the Filipino ability to transform the overlooked into something extraordinary, cementing Pampanga's title as the Culinary Capital of the Philippines.
