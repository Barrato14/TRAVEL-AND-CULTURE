MassKara Festival

Held every October in Bacolod City, the MassKara Festival is one of the most vibrant and colorful celebrations in the Philippines. The name is a fusion of the English word 'mass' (a multitude of people) and the Spanish word 'cara' (face), creating the phrase "a multitude of smiling faces."

The festival was born out of tragedy in 1980, during a period of economic crisis and the sinking of the MV Don Juan. In a defiant act of optimism, the local government and artists created a festival of smiles to lift the people's spirits. Today, it stands as a global symbol of resilience and the "City of Smiles," featuring street dancing competitions where performers wear heavy, ornate masks adorned with beads, feathers, and sequins.
