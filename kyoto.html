Kyoto (京都)

As the former imperial capital of Japan for over a thousand years, Kyoto is a city where time seems to stand still. It is the birthplace of many Japanese traditions, from the refined Cha-no-yu (tea ceremony) to the elusive world of the Geisha in the Gion district. With over 1,600 Buddhist temples and 400 Shinto shrines, the city remains the ultimate guardian of Japan's spiritual heritage.

Kyoto is perhaps most famous for its harmonious relationship with nature. Whether it is the brilliant pink of cherry blossoms in the spring or the fiery red maples of autumn, the city’s landscapes are designed to inspire contemplation. From the golden pavilion of Kinkaku-ji to the towering stalks of the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Kyoto offers a serene escape into the elegance of old-world Japan.
