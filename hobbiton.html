Hobbiton Movie Set

Nestled in the heart of the Waikato region in Matamata, New Zealand, the Hobbiton Movie Set brings the magical world of J.R.R. Tolkien to life. This 12-acre permanent tourist attraction was the actual filming location for the lush, peaceful Shire in The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit film trilogies.

Visitors can step directly into Middle-earth, wandering past 44 intricately detailed Hobbit holes, including the famous Bag End. The experience captures the enchanting spirit of the films, culminating in a relaxing stop to enjoy a handcrafted beverage at the Green Dragon™ Inn.
