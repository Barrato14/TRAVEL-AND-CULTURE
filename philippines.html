Philippines Natural Wonders

Explore some of the most famous nature spots in the Philippines

⬅ Back to Journey

Mayon Volcano

Mayon Volcano in Albay is famous for its near perfect cone shape. It is one of the most iconic volcanoes in the Philippines and a popular destination for tourists and photographers.

Chocolate Hills

Located in Bohol, the Chocolate Hills are more than 1,200 natural hills that turn brown during the dry season, making them look like giant chocolate mounds.

Puerto Princesa Underground River

This underground river in Palawan flows through a massive cave system and is one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Boracay Island

Known for its white sand beaches and vibrant nightlife, Boracay is a tropical paradise offering crystal-clear waters and stunning sunsets.

Coron, Palawan

Coron is famous for its spectacular limestone cliffs, clear blue lagoons, and world-class diving spots with shipwrecks from World War II.

Taal Volcano

Taal Volcano is one of the most active volcanoes in the Philippines and features a picturesque crater lake within a lake, surrounded by beautiful landscapes.
