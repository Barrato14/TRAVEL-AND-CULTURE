Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my booking?

You can cancel your booking by logging into your account, navigating to "My Trips," selecting the booking you wish to cancel, and clicking "Cancel Booking." Please check our Terms & Conditions for the refund policy specific to your ticket.

Are my payment details secure?

Yes, all transactions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL technology. We do not store your credit card information on our servers.

What happens if my tour is canceled due to weather?

If an operator cancels an experience due to unsafe weather conditions, you will be offered an alternative date or a full refund automatically.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Ticket transfers depend on the specific tour operator. Please contact our support team at least 48 hours before your scheduled trip to request a name change.

Still need help?

Our support team is available 24/7 to assist you.
