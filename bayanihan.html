Explore Philippine Culture

In the Philippines, we don't just live alongside our neighbors—we carry them. Bayanihan is an ancient Filipino custom derived from the word 'bayani' (hero). It embodies the spirit of communal unity, work, and cooperation to achieve a common goal.

Whether it’s a village literally lifting a house to move it to higher ground, or modern-day volunteers rushing to help after a storm, it is a testament to the Filipino heart: No one is left behind, and no load is too heavy when we move as one.
