Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

⬅ Back to Home

1. Information We Collect

When you use Travel Explorer, we may collect personal information that you provide to us voluntarily, including but not limited to:

  • Name, email address, and contact details when subscribing to our newsletter or filling out contact forms.
  • Booking details and preferences when you inquire about specific destinations or experiences.
  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and device information collected automatically through cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To personalize your experience and provide destination recommendations.
  • To improve our website, services, and user interface.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, offers, and customer support.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us understand how you interact with our site. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

Travel Explorer respects your privacy. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties without your consent, except to trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information.

5. Third-Party Links

Occasionally, at our discretion, we may include or offer third-party products or services on our website. These third-party sites have separate and independent privacy policies. We, therefore, have no responsibility or liability for the content and activities of these linked sites.

6. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Changes and clarifications will take effect immediately upon their posting on the website. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
