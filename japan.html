Japan Natural Wonders

Explore some of the most famous nature spots in Japan

⬅ Back to Journey

Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is Japan's tallest mountain and an iconic symbol. Visitors enjoy hiking, stunning sunrise views, and photography around the Five Lakes area.

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Located in Kyoto, this bamboo forest is famous for its serene walking paths, beautiful sunlight filtering through tall bamboo stalks, and peaceful atmosphere.

Kyoto

Kyoto is renowned for its historic temples, gardens, and natural beauty. Visitors can enjoy cherry blossoms, autumn leaves, and traditional Japanese landscapes.

Shirakawa-go

Shirakawa-go is a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its traditional gassho-zukuri farmhouses, scenic mountain landscapes, and snow-covered winters.

Nikko National Park

Nikko National Park features mountains, lakes, waterfalls, and historic temples. It’s a perfect destination for hiking and experiencing Japan’s lush nature.

Ogasawara Islands

A remote island chain, the Ogasawara Islands are known for pristine beaches, clear waters, and rare wildlife, often called the “Galapagos of the Orient.”
