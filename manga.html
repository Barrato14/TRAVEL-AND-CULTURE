Manga (漫画)

More than just "comics," Manga is a sophisticated pillar of Japanese culture and a global artistic phenomenon. The word itself translates to "whimsical pictures," and its roots can be traced back to 12th-century scrolls. Today, it encompasses a vast range of genres—from action-packed Shonen to emotional Shojo—appealing to every age group and walk of life.

What sets Manga apart is its unique visual language: the use of "speed lines" to show movement, exaggerated facial expressions to convey deep emotion, and a right-to-left reading format. Since the post-war era, led by the "God of Manga" Osamu Tezuka, it has evolved into a multi-billion dollar industry that influences film, fashion, and literature worldwide, proving that a story told through ink and paper knows no borders.
